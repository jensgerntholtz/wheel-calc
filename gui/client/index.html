Wheel calculator

Division by zero is defined. This is the wheel of fractions over Z/2Z: + is xor, · is and, and the carrier is 0, 1, ∞ = 1/0, ⊥ = 0/0.

In plain terms: ∞ is the value of dividing by zero, and ⊥ means “no value” — the calculation has no answer at all. Ordinary arithmetic stops with an error at that point; the wheel reports ⊥ instead.
